Registered Nurse, Emergency Department

Helsinki Central Hospital — Helsinki, Finland (shift work)

About the role
You will work in a busy emergency department as part of a team of nurses and doctors. You will assess patients on arrival, give medication, monitor vital signs and support patients and their families.

What you must have:
- A registered nurse qualification valid in Finland (Valvira registration).
- At least 2 years of experience in emergency or acute care nursing.
- Basic and advanced life support certificates (BLS, ALS).
- Experience with triage and patient assessment.
- Fluent Finnish and good Swedish, as required for public-sector healthcare.

Nice to have:
- Experience in paediatric emergency care.
- A specialisation in intensive care nursing.

What we offer:
- Shift allowances, occupational health care and a training budget.

How to apply
Send your CV and a copy of your Valvira registration through our recruitment portal.
